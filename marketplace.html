One discovery layer for supply, labor, services, and logistics.

Marketplace lanes

Each discovery lane solves a different coordination problem.

Rather than placing everything into one generic feed, FarmConnecta separates the public marketplace into distinct lanes for products, jobs, services, and logistics.

Products

Supply discovery

Crop and produce listings with category, location, timing, and seller visibility.

Jobs

Labor coordination

Agricultural roles, seasonal work, and field opportunities aligned to skills and geography.

Services

Expert support

Agronomists, veterinarians, mechanization providers, and field specialists.

Logistics

Movement and delivery

Transport, aggregation, and cold-chain support for getting outputs where they need to go.
